## Part A — Master Services Agreement

**1. Agreement.** This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Arkanium and the client named in a Statement of Work ("Client"). Each Statement of Work ("SOW") is governed by this Agreement. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly states otherwise.

**2. Services.** Arkanium will provide the consulting services described in each SOW (the "Services"). Services may be delivered in whole or part by Arkanium personnel and by automated agents operated under Arkanium's supervision, as described in Section 9.

**3. Statements of Work.** Each SOW states the scope, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, fees, payment schedule and any assumptions. No work starts before a SOW is signed by both parties.

**4. Changes.** Changes to scope, fees or schedule require a written change order signed by both parties. Arkanium is not obliged to perform changes until a change order is signed.

**5. Fees and payment.** Fees are stated in US dollars in each SOW. Fixed-fee work is invoiced by milestone, with any deposit stated in the SOW. Retainer services are invoiced monthly in advance. Time-and-materials work is invoiced periodically up to any stated cap. Invoices are payable within [15/30] days of issue. Payments are made through the payment page provided with each invoice.

**6. Late payments.** Amounts not paid when due accrue interest at [the lower of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by New Mexico law]. Arkanium may suspend Services for invoices more than [30] days overdue after written notice.

**7. Taxes.** Fees exclude taxes. Client is responsible for applicable sales, use and similar taxes, except taxes on Arkanium's income. Where Arkanium is required to collect a tax, it will state it on the invoice.

**8. Intellectual property.**
(a) *Deliverables.* Upon Arkanium's receipt of full payment for a deliverable, Arkanium assigns to Client the rights to that deliverable as stated in the SOW, excluding Arkanium Materials.
(b) *Arkanium Materials.* Arkanium retains all rights in its pre-existing and general materials, including its methodology and framework, templates, playbooks, tools, know-how, and any improvement to them (together, "Arkanium Materials"). Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use Arkanium Materials embedded in a deliverable, solely for Client's internal business purposes.
(c) *Internal case records.* Client grants Arkanium a perpetual, royalty-free license to store, analyze and use records of the engagement internally to improve its services, in the form selected in the SOW: [Option 1: full record, including Client name and specifics] or [Option 2: anonymized record with identifying information removed]. [Decision required: Option 1 or Option 2 per client, per Section 3 of ARK-LEG-001.] Arkanium will not disclose Client's identity or Confidential Information outside Arkanium's personnel and systems.
(d) *Feedback.* Client may provide suggestions. Arkanium may use them without restriction.

**9. Use of automated agents.** Client acknowledges that Arkanium uses automated agents to prepare drafts, analyses and recommendations. All deliverables are reviewed by Arkanium personnel before release. Arkanium personnel are responsible for the content of deliverables. Automated agents do not sign documents, make commitments on Arkanium's behalf, or communicate with Client without Arkanium's review. Client will receive notice of this practice in each SOW.

**10. Results.** Arkanium describes expected outcomes in good faith. Expected outcomes are not guarantees. Results are measured and reported as stated in each SOW.

**11. Confidentiality.** Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own, and will use it only to perform this Agreement. Exceptions: information that is public, already known, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party, and disclosures required by law (with prompt notice where lawful). Obligations survive for [three] years after termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets. The Mutual NDA (ARK-LEG-003) applies to pre-contract discussions.

**12. Data protection.** Each party complies with applicable privacy laws. Where Arkanium processes personal data on Client's behalf, the Data Processing Addendum (ARK-LEG-003, Part B) applies.

**13. Warranties.** Arkanium will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry practices. Except as stated in this Section, Arkanium disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

**14. Client responsibilities.** Client will provide timely access to information, systems and personnel reasonably needed, and will make decisions that the SOW assigns to Client. Client is responsible for the accuracy of information it provides. Client is responsible for its own legal, tax, financial and regulatory decisions. Arkanium's services are not legal, tax, financial or investment advice.

**15. Acceptance.** Each deliverable is accepted as stated in the SOW. If Client does not respond within the review period stated in the SOW, the deliverable is deemed accepted. Client may submit corrections within that period; Arkanium will address corrections that fall within the SOW scope.

**16. Limitation of liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by law: (a) neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or lost profits; and (b) each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable under the applicable SOW in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim. These limits do not apply to breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, payment obligations, or liability that cannot be limited by law.
